# Briefs show evidence, never scores

A Brief lists sourced evidence per Criterion, unknowns and follow-up questions, and deliberately contains no score or recommendation. A score invites rubber-stamping, a wrong judgement about a real founder causes real harm, human oversight of selection decisions is expected under the EU AI Act, and "is every claim backed by its source?" can be evaluated where "is 7/10 right?" cannot. A deterministic eval enforces that no Brief contains a score.
